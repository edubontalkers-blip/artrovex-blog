About Body Advice

Body Advice is an independent wellness blog dedicated to practical, honest guidance on joint care, muscle recovery, massage techniques and daily movement. We publish new content every day in five languages: English, German, Italian, Spanish and French.

What We Cover

We write about the things that matter for people who want to stay active and take care of their bodies — joint mobility, muscle care, massage routines, warm-up and cool-down techniques, and the small daily habits that make a real difference over time.
