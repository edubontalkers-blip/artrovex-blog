Why Your Lower Back Still Aches After Rest — And What I Do About It

Last winter I pulled a 14-hour training day covering six back-to-back client sessions, and by the time I got home my lower back felt like wet concrete. Not sharp pain — just that heavy, dead exhaustion that sits right in the spine's core. I knew exactly what was screaming at me: the multifidus, those small deep muscles that nobody talks about until they're done.

What the Multifidus Actually Does

The lumbar multifidus runs along each side of your spine in short diagonal segments, stitching vertebra to vertebra. Its job is postural micro-control — tiny, constant corrections that keep your spine honest during every movement. When it fatigues, you don't feel a pop or a crack. You feel a slow, creeping heaviness that makes sitting, standing, and lying down all equally miserable.

Why Standard Massage Often Misses It

Most foam rolling hits the erector spinae — the long, surface muscles — and people assume that's good enough. The multifidus sits deeper and medially, close to the spinous processes. You need slow, deliberate thumb or knuckle pressure angled inward, not broad rolling strokes. I spend about four minutes per side using small circular movements about two finger-widths off the midline.

My Actual Post-Session Routine

I always start with two to three minutes of gentle cat-cow to bring blood flow in before touching the tissue. Then I apply my warming cream and work the tissue with sustained pressure, moving millimeter by millimeter upward from L5 toward L1. The warmth from the cream lets me sink in without the muscle bracing against me. I finish with a five-minute side-lying rest, knees bent, to let the tissue settle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my multifidus is actually fatigued and not something more serious?

Deep bilateral aching that builds gradually after prolonged sitting or standing, with no shooting sensation down the leg, is often multifidus fatigue — but if you notice leg numbness, tingling, or symptoms that worsen lying down, get a professional assessment first.

Where can I buy original Hondrocream and how do I avoid fakes?

The safest place I've found online is the official eBay listing from the verified manufacturer's store — look for the seller's verified badge, check that the product packaging matches the official Hondrocream branding, and avoid any listing offering suspiciously large quantity discounts or unofficial bundle deals. Counterfeit warming creams often have a thin, watery texture and no real warmth on application — genuine Hondrocream warms immediately.

Can I do this massage every day?

I do a lighter version daily and a deeper session two or three times a week — daily gentle work on the multifidus keeps the tissue from stiffening, but overly aggressive daily deep pressure can leave the area sore, so listen to your body.
