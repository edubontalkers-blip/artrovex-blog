Shoulder tension during the day: movement and comfort tips

Raised or tense shoulders can accompany stress, prolonged desk work or other factors. One muscle is not necessarily responsible for discomfort.

Shoulder movement

The trapezius is one of several muscles that help move and position the shoulder blades. Posture alone does not reliably explain pain.

Gentle changes

Vary your position, take short movement breaks and adjust work tasks to your comfort. Avoid forcing the shoulders downward or exercising through pain.

When to seek advice

Seek medical evaluation for persistent pain, new weakness, numbness, injury-related symptoms or difficulty moving an arm.
