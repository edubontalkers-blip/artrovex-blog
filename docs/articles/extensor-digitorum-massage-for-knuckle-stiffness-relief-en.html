What Is the Extensor Digitorum and Why Does It Get So Tight?

The extensor digitorum is a forearm muscle that fans out into tendons over each finger knuckle. When you type, grip, or even just carry shopping bags repeatedly, it shortens and hardens without you noticing. Most people massage their palms when hands feel stiff — but the real tension often sits on top, along those tendons. That's where focused work makes a visible difference.

A Simple Massage Technique I Use Every Morning

I start by placing my thumb at the base of the forearm, just below the elbow on the outer side, and slowly drag it toward each knuckle along each tendon line. Short, firm strokes work better than long sweeping ones here. I spend about sixty seconds per hand — unhurried, deliberate. You feel the tissue soften surprisingly fast once you're consistent.
