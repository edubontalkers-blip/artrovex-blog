My Toes Were Curled Like Claws — Here's the Massage Routine That Changed My Mornings

Last winter, I stepped out of bed and my toes curled so tight I nearly stumbled into the doorframe. After decades of sprinting on tracks and now standing on gym floors coaching, my toe flexors had basically decided they were done cooperating. I'm Marco, 48, ex-middle-distance runner from Rome, and I want to share exactly what I do now every single morning.

Why Toe Flexors Get So Tight

The flexor digitorum brevis and longus run underneath your foot and pull the toes downward. Hours in shoes, years of athletic loading, or even long desk sessions compress these tissues gradually. Over time, the toes start resisting full extension first thing in the morning. It's not dramatic — just stiff, curled, and slightly miserable.

The Massage Technique I Use Daily

I sit on the edge of the bed and cross one foot over my knee. Using my thumb, I work slowly from the ball of the foot toward each toe, pressing into the plantar surface with firm circular strokes. I spend about 90 seconds per foot, paying extra attention to the space between the metatarsals. Short strokes first, then longer sweeping ones — that order matters to me.
