What to consider

Discomfort can have different causes. Avoid forcing movements or applying strong pressure to painful areas. Adjust activities according to comfort and seek qualified advice for persistent or worsening symptoms.

When to seek help

Seek prompt medical advice for severe pain, new weakness, numbness, significant swelling, fever or symptoms following an injury. Emergency symptoms require urgent care.
