My Vastus Medialis Massage Routine After Years of Inner Knee Discomfort

Three years ago, I hobbled off a training pitch in Madrid with my right knee swollen and angry — I thought my coaching days were done. The surgeon pointed at the vastus medialis on the scan and said, matter-of-factly, 'This muscle has been neglected for years.' That single sentence changed how I approach every warm-down session I run today.

What Exactly Is the Vastus Medialis and Why Does It Matter?

The vastus medialis is the teardrop-shaped muscle sitting on the inner side of your thigh, just above your kneecap. It plays a huge role in keeping the kneecap tracking properly during movement. When it gets tight or underworked, you start to feel that nagging inner knee cap discomfort that is so easy to dismiss. I dismissed it for two full seasons — big mistake.

How I Actually Massage the Vastus Medialis

I sit on the floor with my leg extended and find the soft, fleshy area roughly four fingers above and slightly inward from the top of the kneecap. Using my thumb or a massage ball, I apply slow, firm circles — not aggressive digging. I work from the lower part of the muscle upward toward the inner thigh, spending about ninety seconds on each spot before moving. Consistency matters far more than pressure here.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I massage my vastus medialis?

I personally do it four to five times a week, usually after training or in the evening. Daily is fine for most people, but listen to how your body responds — more is not always better.

Can I use any massage cream or does it have to be Artrovex?

You can use any cosmetic massage cream with good slip. I prefer Artrovex because the two-phase cooling-then-warming effect gives me a clear sense that something is happening, which keeps me focused during the massage.

Is this massage suitable for someone who has never had a knee injury?

Absolutely — plenty of my athletes massage this area purely as maintenance. You do not need an injury history to benefit from keeping the vastus medialis supple and well-cared for.

Try adding five minutes of vastus medialis massage to your evening routine this week — your future self will probably thank you.
