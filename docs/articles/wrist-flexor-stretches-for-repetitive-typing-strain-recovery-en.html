Wrist discomfort after typing: practical desk habits

Long typing sessions can be associated with wrist discomfort, but symptoms have different causes. These general tips do not replace assessment.

Comfortable workstation

Keep wrists in a comfortable, relatively neutral position. Adjust chair, keyboard and mouse so you do not need to reach or bend awkwardly. There is no single ideal posture for everyone.

Movement breaks

Change position regularly and take brief breaks from repetitive tasks. Gentle wrist movement within a comfortable range may help you stay mobile; do not force stretches or work through pain.

When to get help

Seek professional advice for persistent pain, tingling, numbness, weakness, swelling or symptoms that disrupt sleep or daily tasks.
