What Is the Multifidus and Why Should You Care About It

The multifidus is a group of small, deep muscles that run along your spine, and they're basically invisible until they stop doing their job. They don't look impressive on any anatomy chart. But they are responsible for keeping each vertebra stable during almost every movement you make. When they're underactivated — which happens more than people realize — your lumbar spine starts compensating in all the wrong ways.

Three Exercises I Actually Do Every Morning

Bird-dog is my non-negotiable starting point: on all fours, I extend one arm and the opposite leg, hold for five seconds, and switch. Dead bugs come next — lying on my back, I lower alternating limbs while keeping my lower back completely pressed into the floor. I finish with prone hip extensions, just lifting one leg slightly off the floor while keeping the pelvis still. These three together take maybe twelve minutes and the cumulative effect over weeks is real.
