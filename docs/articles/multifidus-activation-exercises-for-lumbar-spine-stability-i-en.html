Lower back comfort and movement: practical guidance

Lower back discomfort has many possible causes. Gentle movement and a gradual return to everyday activities can be helpful for many people, but no single exercise suits everyone.

Understanding back discomfort

The multifidus is one of several muscles supporting movement and control of the spine. Pain does not necessarily mean that a specific muscle is weak or damaged.

Comfortable movement

Try short walks or gentle changes of position as tolerated. Increase activity gradually and avoid movements that worsen symptoms. A clinician can suggest suitable exercises if pain persists.

When to seek medical care

Seek urgent medical attention for new bladder or bowel dysfunction, numbness around the groin, or significant progressive leg weakness. Discuss persistent or worsening symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional.
