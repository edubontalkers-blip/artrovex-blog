Inner foot arch discomfort after walking: everyday guidance

Discomfort along the inner arch of the foot can have different causes. This article explains simple ways to adjust activity without claiming a specific diagnosis.

What the inner arch does

The foot arch is supported by bones, ligaments, muscles and tendons, including the posterior tibial tendon. Pain alone cannot identify which structure is involved.

Everyday comfort

Choose comfortable, supportive footwear and adjust walking distance according to symptoms. Avoid forceful massage over painful or swollen areas and do not push through worsening pain.

When to seek help

Get professional advice if discomfort persists, worsens, follows an injury, or is accompanied by swelling, numbness or difficulty bearing weight.
