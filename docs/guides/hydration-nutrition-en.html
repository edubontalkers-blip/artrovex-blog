Everyday hydration and balanced eating

Practical everyday guidance grounded in WHO, EFSA and NHS publications.

How much should you drink?

The NHS suggests 6–8 cups or glasses of fluid daily as a general guide. More may be needed in hot weather or with prolonged activity. Other drinks and foods also contribute fluid.

EFSA adequate total water intake estimates are about 2.0 litres daily for adult women and 2.5 litres for adult men. These figures include drinks AND water in food, not a requirement to drink that much plain water.

Eat a varied diet

WHO recommends at least 400 g of fruit and vegetables daily from age 10. Whole grains, legumes and nuts support dietary variety. Adults should consume less than 5 g salt per day.

This is general information; follow individual medical advice if you have specific fluid restrictions.
