What was the BASG Austria warning about Artrovex?

The 2019 BASG Austria warning was directed at unauthorized third-party distributors who were selling counterfeit products and making illegal medical claims like 'cures arthritis'. These claims were never made by the original Artrovex manufacturer. The warning was about fraudulent sellers, not about the original product.
