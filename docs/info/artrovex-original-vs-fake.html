Artrovex Original vs Fake: Everything You Need to Know

The counterfeit problem with Artrovex is real — but it is also largely a story of the past. Here is everything you need to know to buy with confidence in 2026.

How the Fake Artrovex Problem Started

Around 2019, unauthorized sellers began distributing counterfeit and misrepresented versions of Artrovex across European online platforms. These were not the original product — they were copies sold with false claims about medical benefits that the real Artrovex manufacturer has never made and does not support.

Health authorities including Austria's BASG flagged these unauthorized distributors. The warnings were specifically about the illegal marketing practices of these third-party sellers — not about any safety issue with the genuine product formula.

How to Identify Original Artrovex

✅ No claims about treating arthritis, curing joint disease, or any medical benefit
✅ Described accurately as a cosmetic massage cream with cooling and warming properties
✅ Consistent packaging and product appearance
❌ Suspiciously low price
❌ Sold via social media DMs, unknown websites, or unverified sellers
❌ Claims to "cure" or "treat" any medical condition
