# BODY ADVICE — EVERYDAY IN MOTION

Design system v1 · Germany / Italy first · October 2026

## Editorial signature
A quiet, cinematic European magazine about everyday movement, food, hydration and rest. Avoid generic wellness stock photography, fake expert endorsements, medical before/after scenes and product placement in editorial photography. Originality is achieved through consistent art direction, not by claiming nobody else has used similar patterns.

## Visual language
- Forest ink #183D2F, chalk #F7F6F1, warm limestone #E9E1D5, muted sage #BFD7C6, deep terracotta #A85D44.
- Headings: high-contrast editorial serif; body: readable neutral sans. Headings remain readable without remote fonts.
- Photography: natural window light, authentic candid gestures, ordinary clothing, local urban and domestic settings, visible environment; realistic people of different ages and backgrounds.
- Distinctive recurring motifs: slender editorial issue numbers, vertical category captions, rounded-corner photo windows, small location/time-of-day captions, offset image crops, subtle motion lines.
- Layout: one cinematic lead story, then asymmetric 2:1 story grid, then compact editorial index. No giant uniform walls of cards. Maintain keyboard focus and semantic article headings.
- Motion: optional 120–180 ms hover transitions, no scroll hijacking; honour prefers-reduced-motion.
- Photography: minimum 40 *distinct actual assets* before claiming completion. Each must have a documented source or original-generation record, descriptive localized alt, WebP/AVIF variants, responsive sizes and license provenance. Do not use image-search previews as production assets.

## DE / IT language direction
- German: concise useful headlines, precise terms, avoid exaggerated promises, make sources obvious.
- Italian: fluent local idiom, inviting but restrained headlines, Mediterranean daily-life relevance without stereotypes.
- Other languages supported EN / ES / FR, with their own copy and proper hreflang.

## Content sourcing
- WHO healthy diet: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet
- NHS fluids: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-guidelines-and-food-labels/water-drinks-nutrition/
- EFSA dietary reference values for water: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/press/news/nda100326
- Every quantified recommendation should link to the relevant primary source; distinguish total water intake from beverages. Avoid medical claims about branded products.

## Publication QA
1. Confirm generated page and index link resolve, including all five languages.
2. Verify real images load on 360px mobile and desktop; no stretched subjects, CLS or unreadable overlays.
3. Verify no duplicate photographs in adjacent cards, no product tubes in lifestyle scenes.
4. Check metadata, canonical, reciprocal hreflang, indexability and internal links.
5. Run automated Lighthouse / axe audits, then human visual review. Do not assert awards or uniqueness as facts.

## Implementation status
This is a design specification, not evidence that 40 photos or a redesigned production site have been deployed.
